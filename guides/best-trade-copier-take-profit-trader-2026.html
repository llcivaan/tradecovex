Best Trade Copier for Take Profit Trader in 2026: What TPT Says About Copying

📅 Updated July 2026 ⏱ 11 min read ✍ Tradecovex Team
Quick Answer

Take Profit Trader's FAQ says you can copy trade Test accounts and up to 5 PRO accounts, with the same line for PRO+. It lists NinjaTrader among the platforms you can trade on. TPT's pages don't give a separate copy-trading policy or address bots, so ask support in writing before you run a copier across several PRO accounts.

Checked against TPT's own site and FAQ on October 14, 2026. TPT publishes less detail than other firms here, and this page says where.

01What TPT says about copying

TPT's FAQ answers copy trading directly. For the Test it says you can copy trade your Test accounts, and you can run as many Tests at once as you like. For PRO it says you can copy trade up to 5 PRO accounts, and for PRO+ up to 5 live-market accounts at the same time. TPT's home page describes up to five funded accounts in any mix of PRO and PRO+, so plan for five in total.

The FAQ doesn't address automated trading or bots, and we could not find a dedicated copy-trading policy. An earlier version of this page said TPT bans copying between PRO accounts. TPT's FAQ says the opposite, so that claim is gone.

Independent video, not made by Tradecovex. We did not review it line by line, so where it differs from the firm's own help center, the help center wins.

03Platforms

TPT's FAQ says you can trade on TradingView, Tradovate, NinjaTrader and other platforms. Its comparison page says PRO+ is a live-market account routed through Tradovate. The FAQ doesn't mention a data feed such as Rithmic.

05Setting it up on NinjaTrader

  1. Confirm each Test or PRO account connects to NinjaTrader before you configure a copier.
  2. Keep PRO and PRO+ accounts to five in total.
  3. Keep followers on the lead's side, since counter positions across your accounts are prohibited.
  4. Track the best day per account in the Test, where the 50% rule applies.
  5. Email support before running a copier across several PRO accounts and keep the reply.
