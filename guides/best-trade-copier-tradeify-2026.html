Best Trade Copier for Tradeify in 2026: What Tradeify Allows, and What Trips Copier Users

📅 Updated July 2026 ⏱ 12 min read ✍ Tradecovex Team
Quick Answer

Tradeify allows copying between accounts you own and manage, in the same direction. It says third-party copiers are used at your own risk and gets no support from the help center. NinjaTrader connects to Tradeify through Tradovate only. The rule that catches copier users is hedging: opposite positions across your own accounts count, even a mini against a micro, so a copier must never leave one follower on the other side of the lead.

Checked against Tradeify's help center on October 14, 2026.

01What Tradeify says about copying

Tradeify's Tradovate guide says you may group trade only between accounts you own and manage, and that copying other strategies or copy trading with other people isn't permitted. It allows third-party copiers but says you use them at your own risk and that it doesn't support or answer for their errors. Its hedging article adds that same-direction positions across your own accounts are allowed.

The compliance FAQ is stricter at the edges. It lists synchronized trading, third-party account management and prohibited trade copying among coordinated activities that may be reviewed, while saying that trade similarity alone doesn't establish coordination. The working rule is one person, accounts in your own name, same direction.

You can hold up to five funded accounts at once across Growth, Select and Lightning. We could not find a separate cap on the number of copied accounts or a combined-balance limit in the pages we read.

03How NinjaTrader connects

Tradeify's supported-platforms page lists Tradovate, NinjaTrader and TradingView under the Tradovate connection, and Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart and R|Trader under Rithmic. Its FAQ says NinjaTrader connects through Tradovate only, using your Tradovate credentials, so a Tradeify account that sits on the Rithmic side can't be used in NinjaTrader. You can't switch brokers on an account without buying a new one, so choose the connection when you buy.

That fixes the copier question for NinjaTrader users: the add-on runs on your machine and sends orders through the Tradovate connection that NinjaTrader already holds for each account.

04Tradovate's own group trading

If you trade inside Tradovate rather than NinjaTrader, its group trading is the free option and Tradeify's guide documents it. The limits Tradeify lists are worth knowing before you choose it: ATM bracket strategies aren't supported while group trading, the Exit All and Cancel All button only affects the selected account, and each account receives an equal number of contracts, with order size a multiple of the group's total quantity.

It lives in Tradovate, so it won't help if your charts, DOM or NinjaScript tools are in NinjaTrader. It also only groups Tradovate accounts, so it can't copy to other firms.

05The rules a copier can break

HedgingOpposite positions in the same instrument, or two products in the same group (ES against NQ, a mini against a micro), are prohibited within one account or across your accounts. 06Setting it up on NinjaTrader

  1. Buy accounts on the Tradovate connection, and confirm each one logs into NinjaTrader before you configure anything.
  2. Trade the lead from one account you own. Keep every follower in the same direction as the lead.
  3. Don't flatten followers by hand while the lead is trading. If you must, close the lead first so nothing can be left opposite.
  4. Check follower trade durations separately. The 10-second rule is applied per account.
  5. Dry-run on micros for a full session before you scale.
