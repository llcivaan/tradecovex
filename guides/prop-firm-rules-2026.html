Prop Firm Rules 2026: Six Futures Firms on the Same 50K Numbers

📅 Updated April 2026 ⏱ 16 min read ✍ Tradecovex Team
Quick Answer

On a 50K evaluation, Topstep, Apex, MyFundedFutures, Tradeify and Take Profit Trader all use a $3,000 profit target and about a $2,000 drawdown. They differ on how fast your first payout can arrive, whether the drawdown trails intraday, how a consistency rule is applied, and what each says about copy trading. Bulenox is the exception on numbers: its own pages show a $2,250 drawdown on Fast Track and Momentum. Each firm's own guide has the detail. This page is the side-by-side.

Every figure was checked against the firm's own pages on October 14, 2026. Where a firm's public pages don't say, the cell says so.

Kimmel Trading's take on the top futures prop firms. It's an independent video and its picks are its own, so use the rule tables below for the numbers.

04Copy trading, firm by firm

If you run several accounts, this is the rule that matters most, and it's the one most often misreported.

FirmWhat the firm's own pages say
TopstepA trade copier is allowed up to $750K of buying power. Hedging across accounts and coordinated trading with other people are prohibited.
ApexBans copying with other traders and says nothing explicit about your own accounts, so get written confirmation from Apex support.
MFFUAllowed across all account types. You take responsibility and MFFU gives no copier support. Copy-trading other traders is not allowed, and hedging is prohibited.
TradeifyAllowed between accounts you own and manage, in the same direction. No third-party copier support. Opposite positions across your accounts are hedging.
TPTThe FAQ lists copy trading as allowed, up to 5 PRO accounts. Ask support to confirm in writing.
BulenoxNo policy found. A $100 monthly fee applies to Third-Party API or Trader Composite connections, and third-party algorithms need management approval.

Across firms, the pattern is the same. Same person, own accounts, same direction is the case that's allowed. Opposite positions across your accounts is hedging everywhere, and copying someone else is banned almost everywhere.

05Choosing between them

  • You want the fastest first payout: MFFU Rapid (24 hours after the first trade) or TPT PRO (day one) are the quickest on paper, and both need a buffer built first. Check the intraday drawdown on both before you count on it.
  • You want the fewest consistency headaches once funded: Topstep Standard, MFFU Rapid and Pro, Tradeify Select and TPT have none after the evaluation. Apex applies 50%, Tradeify Growth 35% and Lightning 20% to 30%.
  • You want end-of-day drawdown: Topstep's loss limit trails the end-of-day balance at both stages, Tradeify uses it everywhere, and Apex and Bulenox let you choose. TPT's PRO account is intraday.
  • You run copiers across many accounts: Tradeify, MFFU and Topstep spell out what's allowed. Apex and Bulenox leave a gap you'd need to close with support.

That's a reading of the published numbers. The right firm depends on how you trade and on what each firm's dashboard shows once you're inside.
