MyFundedFutures Rules 2026: Builder, Rapid and Pro, Checked Against MFFU's Own Pages

📅 Updated April 2026 ⏱ 17 min read ✍ Tradecovex Team
Quick Answer

MyFundedFutures sells Builder, Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro. Every plan has a $3,000 profit target on the $50K and a $2,000 loss limit (Builder also offers $1,500), and none charges an activation fee. They differ on payouts: Builder pays every 48 hours at 80/20, Rapid pays daily at 90/10, and Pro pays every 14 days at 80/20. Rapid and Pro sim-funded accounts also ban trading through Tier 1 news. Copy trading across your own accounts is allowed.

Checked against MFFU's help center on October 14, 2026. MFFU's pages show relative dates, so check the plan page before you buy. An older version of this page described Core, Rapid and Pro; that lineup no longer matches MFFU's pages.

Every MyFundedFutures 2026 rule explained in one walkthrough.

03What changes once you're sim-funded

  • The balance starts at $0. The loss limit is measured from there, and for Builder it starts at minus $2,000 (or minus $1,500 on the add-on).
  • Rapid sim-funded switches to an intraday trailing loss limit. On a $50K Rapid, $2,000 trails your equity high, and it locks once it reaches $100. The limit follows your live equity, so an open profit you hand back still moves it.
  • Pro stays end-of-day. After your first payout, the limit becomes $50,100, $100,100 or $150,100 and stays static.
  • Builder locks permanently once the limit reaches $100 above the starting balance.
  • Contract limits can differ from the evaluation. MFFU lists Pro sim-funded at 5, 10 and 15 minis for the 50K, 100K and 150K, against 3, 6 and 9 in the evaluation. Exceeding a limit can breach the account.
  • Inactivity: Builder and Rapid EOD close an account after 7 calendar days without a trade.

Most requests are approved instantly, according to MFFU. Manual review can take 6 to 12 business hours on weekdays. The buffer is the part that surprises people. It is realized profit you have to build before the first request, so the account is trading with nothing to withdraw until you clear it.

Uses the buffers, minimums, caps and splits on MFFU's payout and plan pages. Daily limits, inactivity rules and manual review are not modelled. MFFU's dashboard decides.

05The consistency rule

MFFU describes it as no single day's profit exceeding 30% (Rapid EOD) or 50% (Rapid, Pro and Builder) of your total profit. For the evaluation it applies to Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro, except the Pro one-day add-on. Builder has no evaluation test and applies 50% in the sim-funded stage instead.

Breaking it doesn't breach the account. MFFU says you keep trading more days until the ratio is met. MFFU's own worked example uses the profit target as the base: on a $3,000 target, a 50% rule means no more than $1,500 in one day. Its article doesn't spell out whether the base moves once you pass the target, so use the payout checker above as a guide and confirm in your dashboard.

06News, hours and prohibited practices

  • News: Tier 1 news trading is banned on Rapid and Pro sim-funded accounts. All evaluations and Builder allow it. The window is no positions or orders, limit orders included, from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after the release. For an 8:30 release that means flat by 8:28:00 and nothing new until 8:32:00. Tier 1 means FOMC meetings, FOMC minutes, the Employment Report and CPI for everyone, plus EIA for energy and agricultural reports for ag traders.
  • Hours: trading opens at 6:00 PM ET and open positions are auto-closed at 4:10 PM ET. Re-submitting orders after 4:10 PM can fill and disqualify the account. On half-day holiday closes there is no auto-liquidation, so you must flatten yourself.
  • Hedging: MFFU prohibits hedging of any kind, defined as holding buy and sell positions on the same underlying at once. Its example is E-mini NQ against Micro NQ.
  • Prohibited: high-frequency trading, exploiting simulated fills, multiple limit orders at the same price to manipulate fills, and coordinating identical or opposite trades across unconnected accounts. Breaches can mean termination, forfeited profits and a review of passed evaluations.

07Copy trading and how many accounts

MFFU's help center says it allows copy trading across all account types, and names Tradesyncer, the Tradovate group copier and external copiers. It also says you take full responsibility for the results and that MFFU won't provide support or resets for copier problems. Separately, its fair-play article bars copy-trading other traders and having two people on the same device, so copying between accounts you own is the allowed case.

You can hold up to five active sim-funded accounts if they are all 25K or 50K, or up to three if any is a 100K or 150K. Each account has its own buffer, payout cycle and loss limit, so a copier doesn't pool anything.

08The Pro path to a live account

Pro has a defined route to live capital. It starts after 3 consecutive payouts, or when profits pass the $100,000 payout cap. Funding runs from $2,000 to $5,000 on a 50K, $3,000 to $7,500 on a 100K, and $4,500 to $10,000 on a 150K, with a static balance. You can only withdraw above your initial live balance until you've had 20 winning days of at least 4% of that balance plus 3 payouts. Builder has its own route after the fifth sim payout, with daily live payouts, a $250 minimum and 80/20. Sim and live can't be traded at the same time once a live account exists.
