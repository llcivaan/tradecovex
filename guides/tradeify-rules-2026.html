Tradeify Rules 2026: Select, Growth and Lightning, Checked Against Tradeify's Help Center

📅 Updated April 2026 ⏱ 14 min read ✍ Tradecovex Team
Quick Answer

Tradeify sells three account types. Select is a 3-day evaluation with a 40% consistency rule and no daily loss limit. Growth can be passed in 1 day with no consistency rule but a soft daily loss limit. Lightning is a funded account you buy directly. All three are one-time purchases with no activation fee, all use an end-of-day trailing drawdown, and Select and Growth pay out at 90/10. Copying trades between accounts you own is allowed. Holding opposite positions across any of your accounts is not.

Checked against Tradeify's help center on October 14, 2026. Tradeify changed several payout caps on September 1, 2026, so the caps below are the ones listed for new purchases after that date.

Peter Kennedy covers Tradeify's pricing, rules and payouts. It's an independent video, so check the figures below and Tradeify's help center for anything that has changed.

A few details that matter. Accounts bought before the new dashboard launch keep the older $2,500 target on a Select 50K. The drawdown recalculates only at the end of the day and trails up after a profitable day, but Tradeify enforces it in real time against your net liquidation value, so touching it intraday fails the account. You also have to place at least one trade per week, Monday to Friday, on evaluation and funded accounts. A trade can last 5 seconds, and Tradeify says it will contact you before acting on idle time.

05Growth funded payouts

  • 5 or more trading days must be above $100 (25K), $150 (50K), $200 (100K) or $250 (150K). The count resets after every payout.
  • The 35% consistency rule applies, and the split is 90%.
  • Minimum payout is $250, $500, $1,000 and $1,500 by size. You also need a minimum balance, not a withdrawal amount: $26,500, $53,000, $104,500 and $156,500.
  • Caps by payout number on a 50K are $1,500, $2,000, $2,500, then $3,000 from the fourth. On a 100K they are $2,000, $2,500, $3,000, then $4,000. On a 150K, $2,500, $3,000, $4,000, then $5,000. The 25K is $1,000 every time.
  • Accounts bought before September 12, 2025 have a different, slower-rising cap schedule on Tradeify's page.

06Lightning funded payouts

Lightning needs no minimum number of trading days. It has the tightest consistency rule on the site. For accounts bought after September 12, 2025 it is 20% for the first payout, 25% for the second and 30% after that. Older accounts are 20% throughout. You receive 90%, and the minimum is $1,000.

07Check your consistency ratio

Uses Tradeify's formula: best day divided by total profit must be at or below the limit, with commissions excluded. Lightning percentages are for accounts bought after Sept 12, 2025. Tradeify's dashboard decides.

Two points from Tradeify's article. A ratio exactly at the limit passes, and only a value above it fails. And losses raise your ratio, because they cut total profit: a $1,000 best day on $1,500 of profit is 66.7%, and a $300 loss moves it to 83.3%. Missing the rule doesn't fail the account. You just aren't eligible for a payout until more profitable days bring the ratio down, and the calculation resets after each approved payout.

08Hours, scalping, hedging and automation

  • Hours: the market opens at 6:00 PM ET Sunday to Thursday and closes at 5:00 PM ET. All positions must be closed by 4:45 PM ET on normal days and 12:59 PM ET on early-close days. Tradeify closes anything left open without a penalty, but holding overnight or swing trading is prohibited.
  • Scalping rule (funded accounts only): over 50% of your trades and over 50% of your profits must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds. Fail either test and you aren't eligible for a payout.
  • Hedging: opposite positions in the same instrument, or in two products in the same group (for example ES against NQ, or a mini against a micro), are prohibited within one account or across your accounts. A long on one account and a short on another is a violation. Tradeify's automated check needs opposing positions, a hedge lasting over 10 seconds and over $150 of profit from it, but the risk team reviews cross-account cases separately.
  • News: Tradeify's overview page says news trading is allowed at your own risk and points to a separate policy for restrictions around major events. We couldn't read that policy, so check it before you hold through FOMC, CPI or the jobs report.
  • Automation: allowed only if you exclusively own the strategy, don't share it, it isn't high-frequency, and you can prove ownership on request.

09Copy trading and account limits

Tradeify's Tradovate guide says you may group trade only between accounts you own and manage. It bars copying other people's strategies and copy trading with other people. Its hedging article adds that same-direction positions across your accounts are allowed and describes that as how copy trading works. It names third-party copiers but says it takes no responsibility for their errors and gives no support for them.

Its compliance FAQ is stricter about the edges. It lists synchronized trading, third-party account management and prohibited trade-copying among coordinated activities that may be reviewed, while saying that trade similarity alone doesn't establish coordination. The practical line is simple: one person, accounts in your own name, same direction. You can have up to five funded accounts at once across Growth, Select and Lightning. In Tradovate's group mode, ATM bracket strategies aren't supported and 'Exit All' only closes the selected account.

10Moving to Tradeify Elite (live)

Tradeify rebranded its live program as Elite in March 2026. It removed the old $100K simulated profit cap, set no limit on the number of live transitions, and lets each funded account that receives a payout become eligible for its own Elite account. You can hold up to five Elite accounts, with no daily loss limit. For Select, Tradeify says you are eligible for consideration after 3 payouts on one account or 10 payouts in total across plan types. It is a minimum for consideration, and Tradeify picks and contacts traders. An older Select versus Growth article still says 5 payouts, so use the policy for your own plan.
